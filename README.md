# AI Emergency Destination Intelligence & Dynamic Ambulance Routing System

## Problem Statement

During medical emergencies, selecting the nearest hospital may not always be the best option. The nearest hospital may not have the required medical facilities or sufficient capacity to receive the patient.

Similarly, traffic conditions and road distances can affect ambulance travel time.

## Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered decision-support system that helps emergency dispatchers identify suitable hospitals and routes by considering:

1. Emergency location.
2. Type of emergency and required medical facilities.
3. Nearby hospital locations.
4. Estimated ambulance travel time.
5. Hospital service availability.
6. Hospital congestion estimates.

## Expected Output

- List of nearby hospitals.
- Estimated travel time to each hospital.
- Hospital capability matching.
- Top three ranked hospital recommendations.
- Recommended road route.
- Explanation for each recommendation.

## Initial Scope

Build a prototype for a small geographical area using publicly available location information, routing data and clearly labelled simulated hospital operational data.

The system will support human decision-making and will not independently dispatch ambulances or make clinical decisions.
